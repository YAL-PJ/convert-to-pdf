About ConvertToPDFFree

Last Updated: May 2026

Who runs this site

ConvertToPDFFree is built and maintained by Yanis L., an independent developer who also runs FreeMergePDF and FreeCompressPDF. You can find me on Quora and X, where I write about browser-based document tools.

Mission

ConvertToPDFFree helps people convert images and documents into PDF files quickly, without signup, uploads, or watermarks.

How the tool works

PDF conversion runs in your browser on your own device. Your files are not uploaded to our servers — the conversion happens locally and the finished PDF downloads back to your device.

Why I built it

Most online PDF converters require an account, upload your files to a remote server, or add watermarks to the output. I wanted a simpler alternative that respects your privacy and works with common file types like images, DOCX, XLSX, and Markdown.

What you get

  • No account required
  • No file uploads to our servers
  • No watermarks
  • Works on desktop and mobile browsers
  • Supports images, DOCX, XLSX, TXT, CSV, Markdown, RTF, HTML
